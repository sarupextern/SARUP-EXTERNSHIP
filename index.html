01 / The Program

From studio
to practice.

The SARUP Externship gives students the opportunity to step into a professional environment, observe practice from the inside, and learn directly from architecture and urban planning professionals.

For Students

See how practice works.

Spend a focused week learning how professional teams work, how projects move forward, and how the skills developed at SARUP connect to practice.

  • Observe projects and professional workflows
  • Experience office culture and collaboration
  • Meet architects, planners and designers
  • Explore practice areas and career paths
  • Build professional confidence and connections
For Firms

Bring emerging talent into your practice.

Hosting an extern creates a direct connection between your firm and the next generation of architecture and planning professionals.

  • Introduce your firm to emerging talent
  • Build relationships with potential future interns
  • Bring fresh perspectives into your office
  • Strengthen your connection with UWM SARUP
  • Support the future of the profession

04 / Beyond The Externship

More ways
to connect.

We are exploring additional ways for SARUP, firms, faculty and SET to connect students with the profession throughout the year.

01

Internship + Entry-Level Recruiting

Share hiring timelines, internship opportunities and entry-level roles with SARUP students.

02

Architecture Interview Day

Connect employers with students and recent graduates through interviews, portfolio conversations and recruiting discussions.

03

Portfolio + Resume Reviews

Help students strengthen the materials they use to present themselves to the profession.

04

Career + Industry Panels

Share professional experiences, career paths, workplace expectations and emerging opportunities.

05

Mock Interviews + Networking

Give students practice with interviews and opportunities to build professional connections.

06

Office Tours + Course Engagement

Invite students into practice through office visits, networking, design challenges or other experiential learning.

07 / FAQ

Good to
know.

How long is the externship?

The program is a one-week professional experience.

Which dates are available?

January 11–15, January 18–22, or March 22–26, 2027.

Is the externship paid?

No. It is an unpaid educational work experience.

What happens at orientation?

Students meet participating professionals, hear about practice and expectations, network with firms, participate in a Q&A, and have food provided during the event.
